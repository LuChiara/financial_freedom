HOW TO DERIVE EACH NUMBER
- coreSpend: average household consumption spending, MINUS housing (rent, imputed

  statistics office's own equivalence scale. If it has none, use the square-root
  scale (cost ∝ √household size). Show the arithmetic in `derivation`.
- medical: out-of-pocket health spending per household (not insurance-covered,
  not tax-funded), scaled to two adults. If no national figure exists, use
  WHO/World Bank out-of-pocket spending per person × 2.
- otherAnnual: household utilities (electricity, gas/heating, water, waste charges).
- housing.rent: average rent for a two-bedroom home, yearly. Say whether it is
  asking rent (new leases) or rent actually paid (all leases).
- housing.homePrice: average or median price of a home sold, national.
- housing.propertyTaxRate: yearly property tax as a share of value for a primary
  home (0 if primary homes are exempt). housing.closingCostRate: purchase taxes
  and fees as a share of price. housing.mortgage.rate: current average new
  mortgage rate from the central bank or banking association.
  housing.maintenanceRate: 0.01 as a convention unless better data exists.
- kidTemplate.annualCost: the extra yearly household spending one child adds,
  from the equivalence scale above (for example, √3/√2 − 1 ≈ 22.5% of a couple's

  data shows young adults typically leave home much later; explain in a note.
- pension.annual: the public pension a couple aged 30 TODAY can expect, after tax,

- pension.startAge: the statutory age that someone aged 30 today will face, using
  any legislated or officially projected increases.
- lifeExpectancy: keep 90 as the planning age, and cite life expectancy at birth.
